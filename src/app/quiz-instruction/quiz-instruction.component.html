Assessment Guidelines & Role Setup

Review progressive Low-to-High question scaling, timing modes, and keyboard shortcuts before starting.

01. Progressive Levels & Step-by-Step Question Depth

  • Choose a role (Student, Frontend Dev, Full-Stack Dev, or Senior / Lead) to align tracks and default pacing.
  • Every one of the {{ quizService.tracks.length }} tracks includes 16 curated questions and scales volume as difficulty increases:
  • Low (5 Qs) covers fundamentals · Medium (8 Qs) blends 3 Low + 5 Medium · High (12 Qs) mixes 2 Low + 4 Medium + 6 High · Progressive Full (16 Qs) tests the full ladder.

02. Timing, Scoring & Cloud Sync

  • In Timed Assessment mode, you have 30 seconds per question. Unanswered items when the clock expires are marked incorrect.
  • In Guided Practice mode (ideal for Students), the timer pauses so you can inspect code snippets and explanations carefully.
  • Achieve 70% accuracy or higher to pass. Signed-in accounts sync attempt history and bookmarked questions to Cloud Firestore automatically.

03. Keyboard Shortcuts

1 – 4 Select option A, B, C, or D
Enter Submit selected answer or advance to next question
B Bookmark or unbookmark the current question for later review
